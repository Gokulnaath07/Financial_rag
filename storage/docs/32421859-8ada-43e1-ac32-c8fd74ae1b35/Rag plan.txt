# PART 1 — WHAT YOU MUST LEARN (IN ORDER)

This is a **learning checklist**, not a tutorial list.
You don’t need mastery — just *working knowledge*.

---

## 1️⃣ RAG fundamentals (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

**Goal:** Understand what problems each step solves.

You must clearly understand:

* What **RAG** is (Retrieve → Augment → Generate)
* Why LLMs hallucinate without retrieval
* Why retrieval quality > model choice

**You should be able to explain in 1 sentence:**

> “RAG injects verified document context into the LLM prompt so answers are grounded in source data.”

---

## 2️⃣ Chunking strategies (THIS MAKES OR BREAKS RAG)

**Goal:** Know *when* to use which chunking strategy.

You must understand:

* **Header / section chunking**

  * Why financial docs benefit from this
* **Semantic chunking**

  * What embedding-guided splitting means
* Why chunk size ≠ fixed token count

You don’t need to invent chunking logic — just understand routing logic.

---

## 3️⃣ Embeddings (PRACTICAL, NOT THEORY)

**Goal:** Know what embeddings do and where they live.

You must know:

* What embeddings represent (semantic similarity)
* Difference between:

  * **client-side embeddings** (you compute vectors)
  * **server-side embeddings** (Weaviate does it)
* Why cosine similarity is used

You **do NOT** need math derivations.

---

## 4️⃣ Vector search vs keyword search vs hybrid

**Goal:** Understand retrieval trade-offs.

You must understand:

* **Semantic search** → paraphrases
* **BM25** → exact terms, clauses, numbers
* **Hybrid search** → best of both
* Why finance/legal almost always uses hybrid

---

## 5️⃣ Reranking (IMPORTANT BUT SIMPLE)

**Goal:** Know why reranking exists.

You must know:

* Retrieval is recall-heavy
* Reranking improves precision
* Reranking ≠ embeddings storage
* It’s optional but powerful

You don’t need to train a reranker — just know how it’s used.

---

## 6️⃣ Prompt grounding & citations

**Goal:** Stop hallucinations.

You must understand:

* Why “Answer only using the provided context” matters
* How to force citations (page/section)
* Why LLMs should say “not found” sometimes

---

## 7️⃣ Finance-safe answering (VERY IMPORTANT)

**Goal:** Prevent fake numbers.

You must understand:

* Why LLMs should not compute math blindly
* Why numbers must come from retrieved chunks
* Why calculation should be done in code, not by LLM

---

## 8️⃣ API-first thinking (HOW YOU WILL TEST)

**Goal:** Be comfortable with Swagger/Postman testing.

You must understand:

* How a POST `/ingest` works
* How a POST `/chat` works
* How to inspect JSON responses
* Why debug endpoints matter
